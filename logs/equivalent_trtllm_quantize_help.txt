                        Specific quantization algorithms will be searched in
                        auto quantization.The algorithm must in ['fp8',
                        'int4_awq', 'w4a8_awq', 'int8_sq'].You can use ',' to
                        separate more than one quantization algorithms(e.g.
                        --autoq_format fp8,int4_awq,w4a8_awq).Notice: fp8 and
                        int8_sq can't be used at the same time.
  --auto_quantize_bits AUTO_QUANTIZE_BITS
                        Effective bits constraint for auto quantization. If
                        not set, regular quantization without auto
                        quantization search will be applied.You can't set it
                        lower than the num_bits of most aggressive
                        quantization format.For example, if 'int4_awq' is in
                        autoq_format, it can't be lower than 4.0.
